Research

My research focuses on evaluating and advancing robustness and alignment in stakeholder-centric LLM-powered frameworks, with special emphasis on mental wellbeing applications. My work explores ways to understand LLM behavior and capabilities in high-stakes human-LLM conversational tasks, combining techniques from natural language processing, machine learning, social media analysis, data science, psychology, and mental health. Topics I have worked on include

  • NLP/LLM + Mental Health [NAACL 2025, ICWSM 2025, JMIR MH 2022]
  • Human-Centered AI [CHI 2024]
  • Social Media Analysis [ICWSM 2024, COMPASS 2023, ICLR PML4DC 2022]
